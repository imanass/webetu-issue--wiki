# WebEtu 2.5.0 installation problems on Android

## Symptoms

- A full-screen message says the installed app is not recognized and asks you to get it from Google Play: see [Method 1: rooted device](Method-1-Rooted), [Method 2: App Manager over wireless ADB](Method-2-ADB-Wireless), or [Method 3: PC with ADB](Method-3-PC-ADB). Methods 2 and 3 are under testing.
- The Play Store says “not compatible with your device” or does not offer the app: see [Method 4: Play Store link](Method-4-Play-Store-Link). It may not help devices filtered by ROM or certification.
- The same recognition message appears after installing an XAPK from an external source: see Methods 1–3. Sideloading alone did not resolve the reported case.
- You want to test launching offline or use a browser: see [Method 5: offline launch](Method-5-Offline-Launch) and [Method 6: web portal](Method-6-Web-Portal). Both are under testing.

## Security warning

APK and XAPK files obtained outside Google Play may be tampered with. Do not enter university credentials in an app from an unknown source. The XAPK source used by the author was APKure; verify the file and its source yourself before installing. Naming the source is not a guarantee that it is safe.

## Known limitations

- Results depend on the device, Android version, and ROM. A result on one device does not establish that a method works on other devices.
- App behavior may change in later updates.
- This wiki is community-maintained and is not official documentation.
